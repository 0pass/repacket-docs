## Overview

The Domain Information feature allows you to look up any domain and receive a reputation score that indicates the trustworthiness and risk level of an IP address or URL. This helps you make informed decisions about which domains to allow or block in your security policies.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/entities-domain.png" alt="Domain Information lookup" style={{maxWidth: '600px', width: '100%'}} />
</Frame>

## How It Works

You can access the Domain Information tool by navigating to the [Entities page](https://app.repacket.com/entities#domain-info) in your Repacket dashboard. Simply enter a domain name or URL, and the system will analyze it and provide a reputation score.

## Reputation Score

The reputation score indicates the trustworthiness and risk level of an IP address or URL.
