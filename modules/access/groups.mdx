## Overview

Organize users into groups to simplify access control and policy management across your organization. Groups allow you to apply policies and rules to multiple users at once, making it easier to manage access permissions and security settings.

Groups allow you to:

- Create logical groupings of users based on departments, teams, or roles
- Apply firewall rules, protections, and policies to entire groups
