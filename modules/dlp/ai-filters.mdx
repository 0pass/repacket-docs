## Overview

AI Filters leverage advanced machine learning capabilities to detect and protect sensitive data across your organization. These filters use sophisticated pattern recognition to identify sensitive information that may not be caught by traditional rule-based detection methods.

<img src="/images/DLP-ai.png" alt="AI Filters configuration" />

## How AI Filters Work

AI Filters analyze content using advanced algorithms that can recognize patterns, context, and relationships in data. This enables more accurate detection of sensitive information across various formats and contexts.
