## Overview

The Access module allows administrators to manage user accounts and groups, and monitor devices within your organization. This comprehensive access management system provides the foundation for controlling permissions, roles, and security policies across all Repacket features.

Access the main Access page at [app.repacket.com/access](https://app.repacket.com/access) to manage users, groups, and monitor devices.

## Users

Manage user accounts, roles, and access permissions across your organization. Access the Users section at [app.repacket.com/access#users](https://app.repacket.com/access#users).

<img src="/images/users.png" alt="Users management interface" />

The Users section provides a comprehensive interface for managing all user accounts in your organization. From this page, you can:

- View all users and their current status
- Assign roles and permissions to individual users
- Manage user access to different features and modules
- Monitor user activity and access patterns
- Configure user-specific settings and preferences

As an administrator, you can add or invite new users to your organization through the **Invite User** button located in the top right corner of the Users page.

### Seats

Seats refer to the number of people who have an active subscription in your organization. The seat count determines how many users can have active access to Repacket at any given time. You can view and adjust your seat count under the **Billing** tab in your dashboard. Managing seats allows you to control subscription costs and ensure you have the appropriate number of licenses for your organization's needs.

## Groups

Organize users into groups to simplify access control and policy management across your organization. Access the Groups section at [app.repacket.com/access#groups](https://app.repacket.com/access#groups).

Groups allow you to:

- Create logical groupings of users based on departments, teams, or roles
- Apply firewall rules, protections, and policies to entire groups
- Simplify user management by making bulk changes to multiple users
- Create exceptions and overrides at the group level
- Track group-level usage and activity metrics

## Devices

Monitor devices connected to your organization's network. Access the Devices section at [app.repacket.com/access#devices](https://app.repacket.com/access#devices).

<img src="/images/devices.png" alt="Devices monitoring interface" />

The Devices section provides visibility into:

- All devices enrolled in your Repacket deployment
- Device status, activity, and compliance information
